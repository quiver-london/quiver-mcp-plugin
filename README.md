# Quiver

Manage Quiver deliveries, returns, products and warehouse stock.

Connect your Quiver merchant account to search deliveries and returns, check warehouse stock, manage products and locations, and review billing records. You can also get delivery quotes, create or update bookings, and manage stock transfers and restock recommendations. Changes and cancellations can have operational or charging consequences. Quiver provides tool annotations to help assistants identify these actions; confirmation behaviour depends on the assistant. Access uses your connected account. Quiver records action metadata for account administrators but does not retain request or response content or use it to train AI models. A Quiver account is required. Service availability depends on Quiver’s operating areas. Refund issuing, payment collection and background monitoring are not supported.
